Dr. Gwang Jin Kim is a Molecular Medicist (PhD) with extensive expertise in developmental biology, human genetics, and bioinformatics. His academic and professional path spans more than a decade across molecular research and advanced data science, with impactful experience at Novartis, where he contributed to predictive modeling, data engineering, and applied AI for pharmaceutical R&D. He has delivered solutions across transcriptomics, genomics, and computational biology, combining scientific rigor with machine learning, statistical modeling, and large-scale data analysis.

At Nimverta, Dr. Kim serves as Chief Operations Officer (COO), where he orchestrates research and operations to accelerate the company’s mission in peptide-based bone regeneration. He leverages his dual background in life sciences and technology to design efficient workflows, align interdisciplinary teams, and scale scientific innovation into tangible outcomes. Beyond operations, he plays a pivotal role in translating complex research into strategies that attract investors, partners, and talent, ensuring that Nimverta’s vision is not only scientifically sound but also strategically positioned in a competitive biotech landscape.
